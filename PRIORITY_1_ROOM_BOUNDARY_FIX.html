Branch: fix/room-boundary-guards

Priority 1: Room Boundary Guards

Implementation note for invalid stage-room pointer accesses during scroll reloads and room loading.

What was wrong

  • At a left edge, (scroll_x >> 8) - 1 could underflow to room 255 before indexing a stage room table.
  • At a right edge, the collision-map reload path could select one room past the stage table.
  • Every bankN_load_room unconditionally drew a preview column from current_level + 1, including the final room.
  • The prior offset - 1 >= 0 condition was ineffective because offset is unsigned.

What changed

  • Added a room-table upper-bound guard at the start of each stage's collision-map reload helper.
  • Replaced unsigned subtraction checks with offset > 0.
  • Blocked left/right collision-map reload requests where the neighbor room does not exist.
  • Wrapped next-room preview rendering in a final-room bounds check.
  • Updated all five stage banks consistently: bank1.h through bank5.h.

Scope intentionally excluded

Stage 1's room-versus-level indexing is isolated in fix/stage1-level-indexing. VRAM scheduling and WRAM capacity work are separate branches, so this change stays focused on preventing invalid room pointers.

How to Test

  1. From this branch's worktree, run make and open floodstory.nes in Mesen.
  2. For each stage, begin in its first room and hold left until the player and camera are fully clamped. Confirm there is no crash, corrupted tiles, or collision-map change.
  3. Traverse to each stage's final room, then hold right until the player and camera are fully clamped. Confirm there is no crash, corrupted preview column, or wrap into room zero.
  4. After touching either boundary, reverse direction and cross the nearest room transition. Confirm the collision map reloads normally and background seams remain stable.
  5. In Mesen, set a read breakpoint on the room-pointer table if available. At the first and final rooms, verify no table lookup uses an index outside that stage's room count.

Validation

PASS: make completed and produced floodstory.nes.

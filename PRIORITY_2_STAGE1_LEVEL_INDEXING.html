Branch: fix/stage1-level-indexing

Priority 2: Stage 1 Level Indexing

Implementation note for separating Stage 1's global room IDs from its eight level-range IDs.

What was wrong

stage1_levels contains 23 global room pointers, while stage1_offsets and stage1_max_rooms contain eight level ranges. The scroll code hard-coded level_index = 0, then later used a global room value as an index into the eight-entry range table.

This could apply level-zero bounds to all of Stage 1 and read past the range metadata after room 7.

What changed

  • Added bank1_get_level_index(room), which maps a global room to its containing declared range.
  • Derived the local room offset, minimum scroll, and maximum scroll from that range.
  • Replaced the right-edge condition with the local range's final room instead of indexing stage1_max_rooms by current_level.
  • Clamped leftward scrolling to the active range's first room instead of room zero.

Scope intentionally excluded

Neighbor room-pointer guards are in fix/room-boundary-guards. This branch changes only Stage 1 range selection and retains existing room-loading behavior.

How to Test

  1. From this branch's worktree, run make and open floodstory.nes in Mesen.
  2. Play Stage 1 through the eight declared room ranges: 0-7, 8, 9-12, 13, 14-18, 19, 20-21, and 22.
  3. At the left edge of every range, confirm the camera cannot scroll into the preceding range while the player can still move within the permitted room.
  4. At the right edge of every range, confirm the camera cannot scroll into the following range and that the player clamps without a wrap or crash.
  5. Use Mesen's debugger or a temporary watch of scroll_x to confirm it stays between the active range's first and final room offsets.

Validation

PASS: make completed and produced floodstory.nes.

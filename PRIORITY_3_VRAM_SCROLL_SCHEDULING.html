Branch: perf/vram-scroll-scheduling

Priority 3: VRAM Scroll Scheduling

Implementation note for preventing idle frames from continuously queueing background metatile updates.

What was wrong

Every stage called its scrolling handler every gameplay frame. When no update was active, the handler began another four-frame job; a stationary player selected the left-direction job. The renderer therefore continued to fill the VRAM update buffer even when the camera had not changed.

What changed

  • Added scroll_update_x, the camera position from the previous scheduling decision.
  • Resets pending scroll work and the recorded position in dispatch_load_room.
  • Each stage wrapper now calls its metatile scheduler only while a four-frame job is active or when scroll_x >> 5 changes.
  • The scheduler runs once per newly entered 32-pixel metatile column, rather than once per idle frame.

Measured impact

Linker BSS increased from 0x2EB to 0x2ED: a two-byte cost for the stored camera position. No attempt was made to claim a cycle reduction without emulator profiling.

How to Test

  1. From this branch's worktree, run make and open floodstory.nes in Mesen.
  2. Stand still for several seconds in the center of a room. In Mesen's event viewer or trace tools, confirm no repeating nametable writes are issued for scrolling.
  3. Hold against the left and right camera limits. Confirm no new scrolling VRAM writes occur after the camera stops.
  4. Walk continuously left and right across several room boundaries. Confirm the background remains gap-free and no stale columns appear while the four-frame jobs complete.
  5. Enter a new stage or reload a room, then immediately stand still. Confirm resetting the scheduler does not leave a pending old-stage update.

Validation

PASS: make completed and produced floodstory.nes.
